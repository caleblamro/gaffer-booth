Gaffer Booth · redesign

Every screen, one system

The operator UI is a dark production gallery. Anything a viewer would see on TV is a chalk-white board on the pitch. Red only ever means on air. Every screen tells one match: Harbour City v Westbridge Rovers. The camera views are rendered from the match's position data, because the data is synthetic and there is no real footage. Open a card to see it full size.
