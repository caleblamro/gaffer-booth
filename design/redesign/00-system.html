Gaffer Booth design system

Floodlight

Two materials. The product UI is a dark production gallery in a neutral ink with a faint pitch-green bias. Anything a TV viewer would see is a chalk-white board laid over a muted pitch. One accent, the tally red, means "on air" and nothing else. Team colours come from the match data and are the only other hues on screen.

Ink ramp

One neutral family, hue about 160, chroma kept under 0.02, so greys read as considered rather than default and sit naturally next to grass.

Rules

Tally is earned.Red appears only when something is live or needs an action now. A screen at rest has at most one red element.
Chalk means on air.Only viewer-facing graphics use the chalk surface. Operator UI never does, so you can always tell what the audience sees.
Numbers are mono or display.Clocks and fact keys are mono. Big stats are condensed display. Both use tabular figures so columns line up.
